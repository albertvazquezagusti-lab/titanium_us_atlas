How to read the orthopedic measures. A hospital counts as orthopedic if it had at least 11 traditional-Medicare inpatient discharges in an orthopedic surgical procedure group, so volumes are a floor and are lowest where Medicare Advantage is highest. A surgery center counts if CMS's certification record flags orthopedic surgery, which can be out of date. VA and military hospitals are not covered. Insurer shares group CMS legal entities by parent name and are approximate.

Circles sit at each county's main population center, not its geographic center.
